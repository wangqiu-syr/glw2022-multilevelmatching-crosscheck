# Rebuild-2's transcription of the paper's tables (copied)

These two files are copied unchanged from the independent rebuild, the private repository `wangqiu-syr/glw2022-rebuild2`, at commit `0a4a038` on `main` (2026-11-17), where they are `data/paper/table1_sim1.csv` and `data/paper/table3_sim2.csv`. They are rebuild-2's own transcription of Guo, Liu and Wang's (2022) Tables 1 and 3, made from the PDF independently of this repository's transcription (`results/paper_table1.csv`, `results/paper_table3.csv`). The four-way comparison uses them only to check that the two transcriptions agree.

Rebuild-2's result summaries used by the comparison are the copies in [crosscheck/rebuild2/data/](../../rebuild2/data/); they are identical to the files at `0a4a038`. The rebuild-2 repository itself was not changed.
